At every point in time, each gate output is connected to either $V_{DD}$ or $V_{SS}$ via a low resistive path, thus a low outout impedance

The sizing of transistors controls the rise and fall propagation delays, which should be made approximately equal. Traditionally, the PMOS widths are doubled of that of NMOS. This ratio can vary from 1.5 to 2.5

Gates with a fan-in greater than 4 should be avoided, as the propagation time increases as a quadratic in the worst case
