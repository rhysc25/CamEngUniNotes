Differences to Fourier:
Fourier starts at $-\infty$, and represents signals in terms of their frequency content
Laplace transforms start at $0$, and are in terms of the general complex $s$

They are often the same if out signal starts at 0, but with s and $j\omega$ interchangeable
There are some cases, like $e^t$ where the Laplace transform converges but the Fourier transform does not
